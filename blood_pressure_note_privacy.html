This Privacy Policy describes how Simple Blood Pressure Note (Japanese Title: らくらく記録　血圧ノート, hereinafter referred to as "the App") handles user information.

The App saves the following health data entered by the user in the internal storage of the device (smartphone, tablet, etc.):

  • Blood pressure (Systolic/Diastolic)
  • Pulse
  • Measurement date and time
  • Other notes entered voluntarily by the user

This data is saved only on the user's device and is not transmitted to or stored on servers belonging to the developer or any third parties. There is no feature to transmit data externally via the Internet.

This App is provided completely free of charge and without ads.
